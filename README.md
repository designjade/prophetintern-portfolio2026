# Jade Lam | Brand Design Portfolio 2026

A selection of brand identity, cultural storytelling and experience design projects, presented as part of my application for the **Design Intern position at Prophet, London**.

I'm a multidisciplinary designer exploring how cultural understanding and everyday behaviour can inspire distinctive brands. My practice moves between research, visual experimentation and experience design, translating meaningful ideas into coherent visual identities and touchpoints.

## Selected Work

### 01 / EGGY
**Reframing Cultural Perceptions**

A playful rebranding project challenging first impressions of the century egg through character-led storytelling, unexpected food pairings and a bold visual identity.

*Individual project | Brand identity, cultural communication, art direction and campaign design.*

### 02 / GUTTER
**Behaviour-Led Brand Experience**

A speculative brand exploring how busy office workers might reconnect with their gut feeling during everyday lunch routines.

Developed through research, physical prototyping, visual identity and exploratory AI-assisted digital interactions.

*Individual project | Brand identity, behavioural design, physical prototyping and speculative digital experience.*

### 03 / GLIMMERA
**Collaborative Worldbuilding & Visual Identity**

An immersive fictional world developed for Disney Imaginations 2025, exploring cultural exchange through storytelling, guest experiences and visual identity.

Selected as one of eight finalist teams from approximately 200 entries.

*Team project | My contributions: team leadership, worldbuilding, visual identity and final pitch presentation.*
